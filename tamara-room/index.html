Assumptions in this sketch

  • Platform top at 72 cm so a 20 cm mattress sits level with the 92 cm sill. The exact height is still to be confirmed.
  • Head shelves use the 16 cm left over by a 190 cm mattress. The socket and USB plate are drawn on the side wall.
  • The cabinet over the feet stops at the window reveal. The air duct in that wall is not shown.
  • Wardrobe doors start 40 cm from the right-hand end, so the desk sits in front of a fixed panel.
  • Door: 76 cm leaf hinged on the right, opening against the wardrobe side.
